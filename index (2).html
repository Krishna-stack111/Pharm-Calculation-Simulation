PharmCalc

Pharmaceutical Concentration Simulator

How to use this simulator

1. Standard calculations

  • Pick a unit from the tabs above: molarity, normality, molality or a percentage.
  • Type your known values. Results update as you type.
  • Read the working line under each result to see your numbers in the formula.

2. Conversions and dilution

  • Open Conversions to move between molarity, molality and % w/w.
  • Enter the solution density (g/mL). It links volume-based units to mass-based ones.
  • Open Dilution to find how much concentrated stock to measure for a target solution.
Check units first: molecular weight in g/mol, mass in grams, volume in mL. The app converts to litres where a formula needs it.
For educational use only. Verify critical formulation calculations independently before use in practice.
